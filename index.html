Hoosier Experience Record

Indiana University Bloomington recognizes four broad categories of Experiential Learning — (Community Engagement & Leadership, Professional & Career Experiences, Research, Creative & Project-based Activities, and Study Abroad & Study Away)—with twelve defined activity types distributed across them.

The intensity of each experience is designated as Exploratory, Applied, or Professional to define the time commitment, amount of student autonomy and expertise required, and degree of real-world or career-ready application.

Experiences listed on this record are both curricular (for credit) and co-curricular (not for credit) and were reviewed by IU to ensure that students received mentorship from IU faculty or staff, engaged in reflection, and applied their learning to real-world contexts.
